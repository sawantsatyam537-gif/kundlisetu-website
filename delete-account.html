Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your KundliSetu account and all associated data (email, birth details, report history, coin balance), please email us at kundlisetu.support@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request," using the same email address associated with your account.

We will process your request and permanently delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., payment records for tax/audit purposes, which are held by Razorpay per their own policies).
